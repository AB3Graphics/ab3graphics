05

Acoustic-Panel Production Recovery

A file-based CMYK correction process used to rebuild a delayed acoustic-material production library and obtain client-approved color from an unreliable printer.

Client

Diesel Displays

Year

2023–2025

Role

Prepress supervision, CMYK correction, test printing, file preparation, quality control, workflow documentation and team training.

Approximately 80 overdue designsCompleted in under six monthsPromotion and two salary increases

Challenge

A large stack of acoustic-panel work tickets was nearly a year behind, with some older than a year. Only about five of approximately 80 designs had been partially printed, and the company’s only printer produced a persistent green cast.

Approach

Corrected color in the artwork files rather than changing printer-wide settings. Tested controlled CMYK variations for each design, reviewed the output against client expectations and rebuilt the production library with repeatable file-preparation procedures.

Outcome

Eliminated the backlog and rebuilt the production library with client-approved results in under six months. The work led to a promotion to Prepress Supervisor and two salary increases.
